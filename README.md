## 🧩 Troubleshooting Common Problems

### "Vulkan device not found"

This means your graphics card doesn't support Vulkan, or your drivers are too old. Update your graphics drivers from the manufacturer's website. If the problem continues, your GPU may be too old.

### "ROM not recognized"

Make sure your ROM file is a `.3ds` or `.cia` file. Also, verify it's the correct version of Ocarina of Time 3D.

### Slow performance or stuttering

- Close all background programs.
- Reduce the window size.
- Lower the resolution in the settings menu.
- Update your graphics drivers.

### Black screen on startup

Sometimes this happens temporarily during shader compilation. Wait 10–20 seconds. If it stays black, try switching between windowed and fullscreen mode.
